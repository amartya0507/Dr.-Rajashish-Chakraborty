About

Dr. Rajashish Chakraborty is a senior consultant in respiratory medicine, with over two decades dedicated to the diagnosis and long-term management of chest and lung disease. His practice covers the full spectrum of pulmonary care, from chronic conditions such as asthma and COPD to complex respiratory infections and interstitial lung disease.

Trained in Bangladesh and certified internationally through the American College of Chest Physicians, he combines rigorous clinical assessment with a calm, patient-first approach — a reputation built over years of consultation across Dhaka's leading hospitals and diagnostic centres.

He consults exclusively through the chamber schedules listed below; all appointments and enquiries are handled by hospital front desks.
